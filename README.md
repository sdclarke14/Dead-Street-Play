# Dead Street - play

A 2D pixel-art zombie shooter for the browser and iPhone. Hold the line through 15 stages across 5 locations, find 10 guns, climb obstacle courses, blow up explosive barrels, and fly over the horde with an upgradeable jetpack.

- **5 locations** (Suburban Street, Downtown, Desert Highway, Industrial Yard, Frozen Town), a new one every 3 stages
- **Obstacles:** hop onto crates, rubble and a wrecked bus; dodge barbed wire, fire and toxic sludge
- **Explosive barrels** that chain-react (they hurt you too)
- **Loot** in houses, cars, dumpsters, lockers and rooftop caches
- **Jetpack** with 3 upgrade tiers and homing missiles, plus perk crates

**Play:** https://sdclarke14.github.io/Dead-Street-Play/ (or open `index.html` directly in a browser).

**iPhone:** open the link in Safari, pick Vertical (phone upright) or Horizontal (phone sideways), then Share -> Add to Home Screen for full-screen play.


## New: your screen, your character
- **Choose Vertical or Horizontal** the first time you play (switch any time from the main menu). Vertical fits every iPhone, including the notch and home bar.
- **Customize** skin, hair, jacket, pants, boots or a full outfit. Unlock gear by leveling up, spending gold, or finding it in loot.
- **Earn XP and gold** from kills, searching, and coins on the obstacle courses; **spend gold on permanent upgrades** (life, damage, speed, loot, jetpack fuel). Progress saves in your browser.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard / mouse | iPhone |
|---|---|---|
| Move | A / D or arrows | on-screen arrows |
| Jump / fly (hold in air with jetpack) | W / Up | JUMP |
| Fire | Space or click | FIRE |
| Search car / house | E | USE |
| Missiles (jetpack Mk2+) | F | MSL |
| Switch gun | 1-0, Q / R, mouse wheel | GUN |
| Pause / mute | P / M | pause button |

This repository contains only the built, playable game (`index.html`, one self-contained file).
